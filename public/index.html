Frequently Asked Questions

How does the platform fee work?

When an employer releases payment for a completed gig, GigConnect takes a small percentage as a platform fee. The student receives the rest directly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Pro plans can be cancelled at any time. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Are there any upfront costs for students?

Never. GigConnect is and always will be free for students. We believe in removing barriers to opportunity.

How are payments secured?

We use an escrow system. Employers fund gigs upfront, and payment is released to students only after work is approved.

About GigConnect

Bridging the gap between student talent and real-world opportunities.

Our Mission

GigConnect was born from a simple idea: every student deserves access to meaningful work experience before they graduate. We believe the traditional job market is broken for young people — too many barriers, too few opportunities, and not enough flexibility.

We created a platform where employers can tap into a rich pool of motivated student talent, and where students can earn money, build real portfolios, and gain the experience that opens doors after graduation.
